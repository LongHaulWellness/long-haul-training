# Strength and mobility library

> Reusable sessions the weekly plans point to. These are **examples** for endurance athletes; replace or adjust them with your coach, physio or gym coach. Add more files to `library/` as needed (drills, mobility, hill sessions).

## Gym: foundation session
- **Warm-up:** 10' easy cardio (bike/row) + hip and ankle mobility.
- **Main work,** 2–3 sets each, stopping with 2–3 reps in reserve:

| Exercise | Reps |
|---|---|
| Goblet squat (or back squat once technique is solid) | 3×8 |
| Romanian deadlift | 3×8 |
| Rear-foot elevated split squat | 3×8 per leg |
| Step-ups on a high box | 3×8 per leg |
| Single-leg calf raise, bent and straight knee | 3×12 |
| Pallof press or side plank | 3×30" |
| Dead bug / bird dog | 2×10 |

**Progression:** add load when all sets feel at RPE 7 or below. Later in the season, add plyometrics (box jumps, pogo hops) and eccentric step-downs for downhill running.

## Home top-up: 15'
- Clamshells and side-lying leg raises: 2×15
- Single-leg glute bridge: 2×12
- Plank: 3×40"
- Calf raises: 2×15
- Foot routine (below)

## Travel session: 20', no equipment
Use a bed, chair, bench or stairs. 2 rounds, stopping with 2–3 reps in reserve.
- Split squat (rear foot on a chair once easy): 10 per leg
- Single-leg RDL (hold a backpack or water bottle): 8 per leg
- Step-ups on a bench or stairs: 10 per leg
- Single-leg calf raise on a step, bent + straight knee: 12 each
- Single-leg glute bridge: 12 per leg
- Side plank: 30" per side
- Dead bug: 10

## Foot routine: 10'
- Towel scrunches: 1'
- Short-foot holds: 10×5"
- Single-leg balance, eyes closed: 3×30" per leg
- Heel walk / toe walk: 2×20 m each
- Ankle circles or alphabet: each foot

Tip: attach it to the end of an easy run, so it isn't a separate task to remember.

## Mobility
- **Short daily dose:** 10–20' of hips, thoracic spine, calves.
- **Longer weekly session:** 45–60', focused on what your sport tightens.
